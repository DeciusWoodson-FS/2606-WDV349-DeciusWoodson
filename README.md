## General Information

Sovereign is a Calisthenics fitness application that uses Role-Playing Game (RPG) elements such as leveling up, achievements, and skill tree to transform regular bodyweight excercise into an interactive and engaging journey of mastery. Sovereign aims to to solve the issue that mainstream fitness tracking apps don't; focusing on the mechanics of progressive bodyweight training, form, static holds, and leveling up through the progressions of skills like the planche or muscle up. This gives calisthenics athletes a perfect place to hone and improve their skills, a beginners a starting line to eventually achieve their fitness goals.
